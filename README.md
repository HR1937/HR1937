### Building

**MediQueue**  
Patients don't struggle with waiting. They struggle with not knowing where they are in it.  
A queue system with real-time token tracking, estimated wait times, appointment booking,
and a token swap feature so patients manage their own time without leaving.

**Complaint Express**  
Complaints don't fail at submission. They fail after it.  
Built routing that hits the concerned department directly, locks closure behind proof of action,
and lets citizens reject resolutions that aren't real, reopening the complaint automatically.
A live public dashboard surfaces resolution rates and closure times by department, visible to everyone.
